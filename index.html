🇮🇳 About Independence Day

India celebrates Independence Day every year on 15 August. It commemorates India's independence from British rule in 1947.

Freedom Fighters

Mahatma Gandhi

A major leader of India's independence movement, known for his philosophy of non-violence.

Subhas Chandra Bose

A prominent nationalist leader who inspired people through his dedication to India's freedom.

Bhagat Singh

A revolutionary remembered for his courage, patriotism and dedication to India's independence.

India's Independence Journey

1857

1885

The Indian National Congress was founded.

1930

The Civil Disobedience Movement gained momentum.

1942

The Quit India Movement was launched.

15 August 1947

India became an independent nation.
